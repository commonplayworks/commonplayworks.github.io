HOUSE RULES

Six rules every village follows

  1. Gamify the work, never the worker.No rankings. No monitoring of people.
  2. Helpers draft. People send.Every draft shows its sources.
  3. Your data stays yours.Counts and dates only. Deleted when we part ways.
  4. Everyone can play.Plain list view, keyboard support, reduced motion.
  5. We count our footprint.Helpers run on a schedule, not around the clock.
  6. We follow the best evidence.Universities, standards bodies and associations, reviewed every year.
